Culinary Festive Heritage

The Royal Mithai Vault

Discover the historic origin, ingredients, and sweet trivia behind India's treasured Diwali confectionery.

✨ Kaju Katli (Royal Cashew Diamond)

Conceived in Mughal royal kitchens, prepared with hand-ground Goan cashews, edible pure silver leaf (vark), and slow cardamom syrup. Symbolizes purity and enduring respect.

🥟 Chandrakala & Gujiya

Originating in the Braj and Awadh regions, crescent-shaped pastries stuffed with roasted mawa (khoya), chirongi nuts, and grated coconut, celebrating lunar beauty.

👑 Mysore Pak (Ghee Velvet)

Invented in 1935 by Royal Chef Kakasura Madappa inside the Mysore Palace using besan (gram flour) and boiling pure desi ghee. Melts effortlessly on the palate.

⚪ Spongy Chhena Rasgulla

Rooted in ancient Puri temple traditions and perfected in Bengal, crafted from hand-kneaded fresh cow-milk chhena poached in simmering sugar nectar.
